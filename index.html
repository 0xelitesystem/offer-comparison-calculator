Offer Comparison Calculator

Two offers, side by side, in your browser

Enter the numbers from two job offers and see year-1 total compensation, average annual compensation over your chosen horizon, and a component-by-component difference table. Everything recalculates live. Nothing leaves this page.

Honesty note: this tool contains no market data, no salary benchmarks, and fetches nothing. Every number below is your own input. Comparisons are only as good as the numbers you type in.
